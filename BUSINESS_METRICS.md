## 1. Business Summary

Audityzer-PRO is an AI-powered Web3 security platform that helps teams analyze smart contracts, research protocols, identify risks, and produce structured security reports.

The business model combines self-serve access for individual developers and security researchers with enterprise subscriptions for security teams, protocols, and blockchain-focused organizations.

---

## 2. Market Opportunity

### Market context

The demand for blockchain security continues to grow as more value is deployed on-chain and as enterprises adopt blockchain infrastructure. Security remains a major barrier to institutional adoption, particularly around smart-contract risk, protocol complexity, and incident response.

### Target segments

| Segment | Need | Monetization potential |
|---|---|---|
| Individual auditors and researchers | Faster research and vulnerability analysis | Low to medium |
| Web3 startups | Pre-audit checks, security workflows, reporting | Medium |
| Security firms | Research acceleration and workflow automation | High |
| Enterprises and protocols | Ongoing monitoring, due diligence, compliance-ready reporting | High |
| Bug-bounty programs | Triage, research, and vulnerability validation support | Medium to high |

## 9. Risks and Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| AI output errors | Incorrect security conclusions | Human review, citations, confidence indicators, clear disclaimers |
| High inference costs | Reduced gross margin | Usage limits, caching, tiered pricing, efficient model routing |
| Security and privacy concerns | Enterprise adoption barriers | Strong access controls, data minimization, private deployment options |
| Market competition | Pricing and differentiation pressure | Focus on workflow depth, enterprise features, and security specialization |
| Regulatory changes | Compliance complexity | Legal review, adaptable architecture, jurisdiction-specific controls |
| Dependence on third-party APIs | Service disruption or cost changes | Multi-provider strategy, contractual protections, usage monitoring |
